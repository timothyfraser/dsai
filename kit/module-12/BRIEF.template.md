# Design brief: <the interface's name>

Guide: Course kit, module 12. Copy to ui-lab/BRIEF.md and fill every heading in a sentence or two.
Guide: Lines that start with "Guide:" are notes for you; check.py ignores them, so each heading needs your own words.
Guide: Words before pixels: write this before an agent generates anything.

## Who

Guide: The person who uses it, by role. Not "users". "The maintenance lead, on shift, between jobs."

## Task

Guide: The one thing they come to do, as a verb.

## Decision supported

Guide: What they decide after reading the screen, and the one number or line that decides it.

## Device first

Guide: Phone or laptop first, and why.

## Accessibility bar

Guide: WCAG 2.2 AA unless you say otherwise and why: contrast, keyboard, labels, touch targets (AA asks 24 px; 44 px is the common design minimum).

## Latency budget

Guide: How long a person waits before the screen must say something, and when it gives up.

## States

Guide: What the screen shows in each of the five states: empty, loading, success, error, and stale (an answer missing its source, or an old answer under a new question).

## Scale rung

Guide: One of: demo, team, public. Then one sentence on why.

## Contract

Guide: The page-API contract: the address, what is sent, what comes back, the timeout, and the plain sentence for each failure.
